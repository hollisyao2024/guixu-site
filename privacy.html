归墟 GuiXu 隐私政策

生效日期：2026-11-12 · 适用于 macOS App Store 版 0.2.0 及后续版本，直至本页更新

1. 概述

归墟（GuiXu）是一款在你的 Mac 上本地运行的多平台内容创作者工具。开发者不通过本应用收集、上传或出售你的任何个人数据。

2. 我们不收集的数据

本应用不设归墟账号，不含广告、统计或跟踪 SDK，不向开发者的服务器发送你的账号信息、作品内容、使用记录或设备标识。

3. 保存在你设备上的数据

你添加的平台账号会话、发布任务、作品快照与应用设置仅保存在本机的应用数据目录中；登录凭据使用系统钥匙串保护的密钥加密。诊断日志仅保存在本机，不自动外发。卸载应用并删除应用数据目录即可移除这些数据。

4. 第三方平台

当你在应用内登录或操作第三方内容平台（例如抖音、哔哩哔哩、微信视频号、快手、小红书、今日头条、知乎）时，应用会在该平台的官方网页中直接与该平台通信。这些通信受各平台自己的隐私政策与用户协议约束，开发者不接收其中的数据。

5. 儿童

本应用不面向儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人数据。

6. 政策变更

如果未来版本的数据处理方式发生变化，我们会在变更生效前更新本页面与生效日期。

7. 联系我们

隐私相关问题请通过 https://github.com/hollisyao2024/guixu-site/issues 联系。

GuiXu Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026 · Applies to the macOS App Store version 0.2.0 and later, until this page is updated

1. Overview

GuiXu is a tool for multi-platform content creators that runs locally on your Mac. The developer does not collect, upload or sell any of your personal data through this app.

2. Data we do not collect

The app has no GuiXu account, and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not send your account information, content, usage records or device identifiers to the developer's servers.

3. Data stored on your device

Platform account sessions you add, publishing tasks, content snapshots and app settings are stored only in the app's data directory on your Mac. Sign-in credentials are encrypted with a key protected by the system keychain. Diagnostic logs stay on your device and are not sent automatically. Uninstalling the app and deleting its data directory removes this data.

4. Third-party platforms

When you sign in to or act on a third-party content platform inside the app (for example Douyin, Bilibili, WeChat Channels, Kuaishou, Xiaohongshu, Toutiao or Zhihu), the app communicates directly with that platform through its official web pages. That communication is governed by each platform's own privacy policy and terms; the developer does not receive that data.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

6. Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, this page and its effective date will be updated before the change takes effect.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at https://github.com/hollisyao2024/guixu-site/issues.
