常见问题

需要注册归墟账号吗？ 不需要。应用在本机运行，直接添加你自己的平台账号即可使用。

如何添加平台账号？ 在“账号”页选择平台，在应用内打开的官方登录页中自行完成登录。

我的数据保存在哪里？ 仅保存在本机应用数据目录，详见隐私政策。

如何彻底移除数据？ 卸载应用并删除其应用数据目录。

GuiXu Support

Get help

For problems or feature requests, open an issue at https://github.com/hollisyao2024/guixu-site/issues and include your macOS version, GuiXu version and steps to reproduce. Please do not post accounts, cookies or other credentials on public pages.

FAQ

Do I need a GuiXu account? No. The app runs locally; add your own platform accounts to start.

How do I add a platform account? Choose a platform on the Accounts page and sign in yourself on the official sign-in page opened inside the app.

Where is my data stored? Only in the app's data directory on your Mac. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I remove all data? Uninstall the app and delete its data directory.
